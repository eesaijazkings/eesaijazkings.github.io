Food and all

I personally love pizza, but it varies for multiple different things; eg the cheesiness, the toppings and even the thinkness of the crust and the dough alike. The best pizza for me is the Pizza from 2 Tasty; a small and standalone Peterborough pizza place. The thickness of the crust and base is absolutely perfect and very thick, but with just the right amount of tension when biting down to make it a simplistic delicacy. The cheese and tomato are perfectly balanced to the point where every bite is a flavour of explosion.
